Services

What we frame

Rough carpentry from sill plate to ridge. If it holds up a roof, a floor, or a deck, it's our job.

New construction

Complete house packages — floors, walls, roof systems, and stairs — framed to print and ready for rough-ins and inspection.

Additions

Bump-outs, second stories, and in-law suites that tie cleanly into the existing structure — no sag lines, no surprises.

Decks & porches

Ledger, footings, and framing built to current code, with flashing and hardware done right so it lasts.

Garages & outbuildings

Detached garages, workshops, and sheds — from slab to trusses, sized for what you actually need to fit inside.

Basement & interior framing

Finished basements, new partitions, and closets. Straight walls and square corners make drywall and trim go fast.

Structural repair & remodel

Load-bearing wall removals, beam installs, sister joists, and rot repair — engineered fixes, carefully shored and executed.

Process

How a job goes

No guesswork. You'll know the scope, the price, and the schedule before the first board is cut.

  1. 1

    Walk-through & estimate

    We look at the site or the prints, talk through what you want, and send a written, itemized estimate — usually within a few days.

  2. 2

    Plan & schedule

    Material takeoff, lumber ordered, permits coordinated with you or your GC, and a start date you can plan around.

  3. 3

    Frame it

    Layout checked twice, walls built square, everything plumbed and braced. Site kept clean and safe every day.

  4. 4

    Walk it with you

    We walk the frame together, punch anything you spot, and hand it off ready for inspection and the next trades.

Why Fenner Framework

The part of the house nobody sees is the part that matters most

Tight tolerances

Walls within an eighth, openings checked against the window and door schedule, headers sized to span. Finish work goes faster on a frame that's actually straight.

Clear communication

One person to call — the owner — who is on site and answers the phone. Change orders are written, priced, and agreed before work happens.

Built for the inspector

Hardware, nailing schedules, fire blocking, and bracing done per code and per the engineer so inspections are a formality, not a scramble.
